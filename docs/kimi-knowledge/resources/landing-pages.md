## How to build a landing page from scratch in easy steps?

### Step 1: Define your goal and conversion objective

Every landing page should focus on one clear purpose. Some pages are designed to collect email sign-ups, while others help generate sales, downloads, or leads for businesses trying to improve customer engagement and online visibility more effectively. A focused goal keeps your content simple and helps visitors understand exactly what action they should take without confusion.

Clear conversion objectives also improve your page structure and messaging. When your goal is defined early, it becomes easier to write headlines, choose visuals, and place strong CTAs that guide visitors toward taking action without confusion, while also improving overall user experience and campaign performance across different audience segments.

### Step 2: Identify your target audience

Understanding your audience helps you create a landing page that feels relevant and useful. Think about what your visitors need, the problems they face, and what motivates them to click or sign up for your service, product, newsletter, or special promotional offer online. This makes your message more natural and persuasive while helping visitors feel understood immediately after landing on your page.

Good audience research also improves your design and copy decisions. When you know what your visitors expect, you can create sections, headlines, and CTAs that connect better and keep people engaged throughout the page, while increasing trust, engagement, and the likelihood of converting visitors into loyal, long-term customers consistently and effectively.

Different landing page styles help businesses collect leads, promote products, and improve conversions more effectively. With Kimi Build, users can easily create the following types of landing pages that simplify design, content writing, and customization.

### Lead capture landing page

A lead capture landing page focuses on collecting visitor information like names, phone numbers, or email addresses. These pages are commonly used for newsletters, consultations, webinars, and free downloads that help businesses build strong customer connections and improve future marketing campaigns effectively.

**Example prompt:**

Create a high-converting lead capture landing page that collects user information such as name, email, and phone number in exchange for a valuable offer like a free guide or consultation. The page should have a clear headline, short benefit-driven subheadline, a few key bullet-point benefits, and a simple form with a strong CTA button like "Get Free Access." Include trust signals such as testimonials or logos and keep the design clean and conversion-focused.

### Squeeze landing page

A squeeze landing page is created mainly to collect email addresses by offering something valuable in return. Businesses often provide free ebooks, discounts, templates, or trial access to encourage visitors to submit their information quickly and easily.

**Example prompt:**

Create a squeeze landing page focused on collecting email addresses in exchange for a free resource such as an ebook, template, or discount. The page should include a strong headline, a brief value explanation, a preview of the offer, and a simple email-only form with a powerful CTA like "Get Instant Access." Keep it urgency-driven.

### Click-through landing page

A click-through landing page is designed to guide visitors toward another page, usually a pricing page or product checkout page. These pages help warm up visitors with useful information before asking them to make a final purchasing decision online.

**Example prompt:**

Create a click-through landing page designed to guide visitors to the next step, such as a pricing or checkout page. The page should introduce the product clearly, highlight key benefits and features, and build trust with reviews or testimonials. Include a strong CTA like "View Pricing" or "Start Free Trial" and structure the content to warm up users before they click through.

### Splash page

A splash page is a simple introductory page shown before visitors enter the main website. Businesses often use splash pages for announcements, promotions, age verification, or special product launches that need immediate visitor attention when entering the site.

**Example prompt:**

Create a simple splash page that appears before users enter the main website. It should display a bold message such as a launch, promotion, or announcement, with one short supporting line and a single CTA button like "Enter Site" or "Continue." The design should be minimal, full-screen, and highly visual, with no distractions.

## What makes a landing page truly effective?

A successful landing page is not only about good design. It should guide visitors clearly, keep their attention, and encourage them to take action without confusion. Businesses that generate landing pages with AI tools can save time while still building pages that look clean by following these practices.

-

**Use clear and simple headlines**

A headline is usually the first thing visitors notice when they open your landing page. Clear and simple headlines help users understand your message quickly without reading large amounts of text or feeling confused about your offer immediately.

-

**Design mobile-friendly, responsive layouts**

Most visitors now browse websites using smartphones and tablets instead of desktop computers. A responsive landing page automatically adjusts to different screen sizes, helping users enjoy a smooth and comfortable browsing experience across all devices without layout problems.

-

**Add trust signals like reviews**

Trust signals help visitors feel more confident about your business, product, or service. Customer reviews, testimonials, ratings, certifications, and partner logos show that real people have already had positive experiences with your brand and trust your offerings.

-

**Keep content short and easy**

Visitors usually scan landing pages instead of reading every single word carefully. Short paragraphs, simple wording, and easy-to-understand sections help users find important information faster while keeping them focused on the main goal of the page.

-

**Improve page speed for better results**

Page speed plays a major role in keeping visitors interested after they open your landing page. Slow-loading pages often cause users to leave quickly, which can reduce conversions and negatively affect the overall performance of your marketing campaigns online.

## FAQ

### Can I create a landing page for free?

Yes, a landing page can be created for free using tools like Kimi Build and other AI platforms. These tools provide templates and simple editors that do not require payment to get started. Free versions usually include basic features that are enough for simple business or personal pages. You can always upgrade later if you need advanced options or more customization.

### How long does it take to make a landing page?

A simple landing page can be built in just a few minutes using AI tools or ready-made templates. If you want more customization, it may take a few hours depending on your content and design choices. Preparing your text, goal, and structure in advance makes the process faster. AI tools also reduce effort by automatically creating layouts and sections.

### What is the ideal way to build a landing page?

The ideal way is to start with a clear goal, so your page has a focused purpose from the beginning. After that, use a clean template and write simple, benefit-focused content that speaks to your audience. A strong headline and clear call-to-action should guide visitors toward the main action. AI tools can further simplify the process by handling structure and design.

### What are common landing page mistakes?

Common mistakes include adding too much content, which confuses visitors and reduces focus. Weak headlines and unclear CTAs also make it harder to get conversions. Poor mobile design and slow loading speed can drive users away quickly. Another mistake is not testing or updating the page regularly to improve performance.

### What should a good landing page look like?

A good landing page should look clean, simple, and focused on one main goal. It must include a strong headline, short and clear content, and a visible call-to-action button. Trust elements like reviews or testimonials help build confidence in visitors. The design should also be fully responsive and work smoothly on all devices.
